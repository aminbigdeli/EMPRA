# Calibration Quiz for Human Annotators

## Purpose

This calibration quiz is designed to train and calibrate human annotators before they begin the main annotation task. It helps ensure consistency and quality in annotations across all annotators.

## Practice Item 1

**Text:**  
If you forgot your password, click the reset link on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your email.

**Answer: Item 1**
- **Fluency:** 5 (Excellent)  
- **Imperceptibility:** 1 (Normal)  

**Explanation:** This is clear, natural instructional text. There are no grammatical errors or strange insertions.

---

## Practice Item 2

**Text:**  
The login page not working correct and user try many times but system not explain what is wrong so people confused how to fix.

**Answer: Item 2**
- **Fluency:** 2 (Low)  
- **Imperceptibility:** 1 (Normal)  

**Explanation:** The grammar is poor, but it reads like a legitimate human complaint (possibly a non-native speaker). It does not contain machine artifacts like random keywords or code tags.

**Key Lesson:** Bad grammar does not always mean "Attacked."

---

## Practice Item 3

**Text:**  
To apply for the license, complete the online form. Discount offer buy now free shipping deal today.

**Answer: Item 3**
- **Fluency:** 4 (Good)  
- **Imperceptibility:** 0 (Attacked)  

**Explanation:** The first sentence is fluent. However, the second sentence ("Discount offer buy now free shipping deal today.") is a spam-like insertion that has nothing to do with "licenses."
